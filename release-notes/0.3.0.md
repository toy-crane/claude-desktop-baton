Usage in the menu bar, and the app now updates itself.

- **Usage at a glance in the menu bar.** Without opening the panel, the menu bar shows the signed-in account's Session and Week usage as a two-bar meter with the Session percent. When a limit stops you, it shows how long until you can use Claude again. If usage cannot be refreshed, the last known values stay visible, dimmed.
- **Updates in a couple of clicks.** About once a day the app checks for a new version. When one is out, the menu bar item gets a small dot and the panel shows **Update available** with **Install…**; nothing pops up on its own. Install… shows the release notes, then downloads, verifies, replaces the app in place, and reopens it. **Check for Updates…** in the `⋯` menu checks right away. Saved accounts, the Keychain approval, launch at login, and the `claude-desktop-baton` command carry over, and an update never restarts the app in the middle of a switch.

**Coming from 0.2.0 or earlier:** those versions cannot update themselves. Download the DMG once and drag the app over the old one in Applications; from 0.3.0 on, updates arrive in the app.
