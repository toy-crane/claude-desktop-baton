Weekly resets in the panel, and a menu bar you can set up.

- **See when each limit resets.** In the panel, each limit now has its own line: its name, when it resets, and the percent used, with the bar right under it. A reset within a day shows the time until it (`↻ 1h 12m`); a later one shows the weekday and time (`↻ Thu 11:00 AM`). A reached limit turns red. Spacing is the same for every account.
- **Both limits in the menu bar.** The menu bar now shows the 5-hour (`5H`) and weekly (`WK`) percent side by side. When a limit is reached, its percent becomes the time until it resets.
- **Choose how the menu bar looks.** **Menu bar** in the `⋯` menu sets what the item shows (the icon, usage, or both) and how usage is laid out: **Columns** (the new default), **Bars** (the earlier two-bar meter with the Session percent), or **Stack** (two lines, each with its percent and reset). Your choice is kept.
