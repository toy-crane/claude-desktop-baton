Model limits like Fable, an easier-to-read panel, and a spinner that turns.

- **See your Fable limit.** When your plan gives a model its own weekly limit, such as Fable, the panel shows it next to the 5-hour and weekly limits, with its percent and when it resets. Hover an account to see how much of the weekly limit that model may use. Accounts without such a limit look as before.
- **A panel you can scan.** Each account's limits now sit side by side in columns, labeled **SESSION**, **WEEK**, and the model's name, with the percent in large type and the bar and reset under it. Account names stand out above their limits, and a thin line separates the accounts you can switch to.
- **See that a switch is working.** The menu bar icon now keeps turning while an account switch runs, until it finishes. While adding an account, it turns only while Claude is being signed out and reopened, and goes back to the usual icon once it is your turn to sign in.
