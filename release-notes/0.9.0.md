The resets badge is easier to hit, and it no longer leaves a highlight behind.

- **Opening resets no longer switches accounts by accident.** The badge accepts a click slightly outside its outline, so a near miss shows the reset details instead of switching to that account.
- **The badge answers the pointer.** Resting on it fills it in, so it reads as something you can click rather than a label.
- **The outline is for the keyboard.** Clicking the badge with the mouse no longer leaves a blue outline on it until the panel closes. Tab still reaches the badge and marks it, and Space or Return still opens it.
